      Reading the card

      1 The label

      What the posting itself says about sponsorship, from its own words only.

      ✓SponsorsSays it will sponsor a visa for this role.
      ✕Does not sponsorRules sponsorship out, or needs citizenship or a clearance.
      –SilentSays nothing on the subject. Not a no.
      ?UnclearSays something, but it is ambiguous. Ask the recruiter.

      Never Sponsors if the posting also has wording that rules it out; that shows Unclear.

      6 What an LCA is

      1. LCA certifiedby the Labor Departmentthe card counts this
      2. H-1B petitionfiled with USCIS
      3. Approvalby USCIS
      4. Hirevisa issued, job starts

      An LCA shows the employer has gone through the first step for that kind of role. It is not a petition, an approval, or a hire, and it does not mean this job will sponsor you.
