Description

We’re building a world of health around every individual — shaping a more connected, convenient and compassionate health experience. At CVS Health®, you’ll be surrounded by passionate colleagues who care deeply, innovate with purpose, hold ourselves accountable and prioritize safety and quality in everything we do. Join us and be part of something bigger – helping to simplify health care one person, one family and one community at a time.

Key Responsibilities

Build and facilitate machine learning and Generative AI solutions across large-scale systems and campaigns, ensuring deployment and updates in collaboration with data engineering.
Develop and participate in presentations and consultations to existing and prospective stakeholders on analytics results and solutions.
Collaborate with internal and external peers and managers to exchange complex information related to areas of specialization.



Required Qualifications

Experience in building end-to-end machine learning models and data-intensive systems in production (2 or more years or PhD with a tech internship as MLE/Software Developer).
Strong background in Data Science with a focus on software development best practices (2 or more years or PhD with a tech internship as MLE/Software Developer).
Proficiency in Python, SQL, and MLOps (3 or more years or PhD with a tech internship as MLE/Software Developer).
Experience in cloud environments (3 or more years as MLE/Software Developer).



Preferred Qualifications

Demonstrated ability to design and build distributed data-intensive products; front-end development and coding in React is a plus.
Experience with Google Cloud Platform (GCP) (2 or more years) is a major plus.
Familiarity with Generative AI and agentic design is a plus.
Proficiency in mathematical analysis methods, machine learning, statistical analyses, and predictive modeling, with in-depth specialization in certain areas.
Experience in Ad-tech is a plus.



Education

Master’s degree in Computer Science, Mathematics, Statistics, Economics, Physics, Engineering, or a related discipline, with 2 or more years of progressively complex related experience (or PhD with a tech internship as MLE/Software Developer).
