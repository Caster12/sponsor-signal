Role Description
Dropbox is seeking a Vice President of Revenue Operations to define and lead the operating strategy for the company’s global B2B go-to-market organization. This executive will be responsible for building a high-performing, scalable revenue engine that enables predictable and efficient growth across SMB, Mid-Market, and Enterprise segments while supporting Dropbox’s evolution across product-led, sales-assisted, and enterprise motions.
This leader will serve as a key strategic partner to the Chief Business Officer and broader executive leadership team, translating company strategy into the operating model, planning frameworks, systems, insights, and execution mechanisms required to deliver durable growth. The role will own Revenue Operations and Marketing Operations across the customer lifecycle, ensuring tight alignment between demand generation, pipeline creation, sales execution, retention, expansion, and overall revenue performance.
The VP of Revenue Operations will have enterprise-wide accountability for revenue planning, forecasting, segmentation, capacity and coverage strategy, incentives, GTM systems, analytics, operating cadence, and revenue productivity. This leader will play a central role in shaping how Dropbox allocates resources, evaluates performance, scales new products and motions, and modernizes its GTM organization through data, automation, and AI.
Responsibilities
Define and lead Dropbox’s global revenue operating strategy, ensuring the company’s GTM model is designed to support long-term growth, efficiency, and increasing enterprise complexity
Serve as a strategic advisor to the Chief Business Officer and executive leadership team, bringing data-driven insights and recommendations to critical decisions around growth, investment, resource allocation, organizational design, and GTM strategy
Own the end-to-end revenue operating model across Sales, Marketing, Customer Success, and Support, including segmentation, coverage models, territory architecture, role design, operating rhythms, and cross-functional accountability
Lead company-wide annual and long-range revenue planning, including bookings targets, quota strategy, capacity and workforce planning, territory design, productivity assumptions, investment scenarios, and resource allocation
Establish a best-in-class forecasting and pipeline management framework that increases predictability, strengthens executive visibility, and enables earlier intervention against business performance risks
Define and evolve the company’s revenue performance architecture, including the KPIs, operating cadences, inspection mechanisms, and executive business reviews used to assess growth, productivity, efficiency, retention, and expansion
Lead Revenue Operations across the full customer lifecycle, creating scalable and integrated processes from demand generation and lead management through opportunity conversion, renewal, expansion, and customer growth
Partner with Marketing leadership to oversee Marketing Operations and ensure strong alignment between demand generation investments, pipeline creation, segmentation, campaign performance, and downstream revenue outcomes
Partner closely with Finance on revenue strategy and planning, pricing and packaging operations, investment allocation, deal governance, incentive compensation, and performance management
Partner with Product and GTM leadership to define the operating strategy for new products, new customer segments, pricing changes, packaging evolution, and emerging sales motions
Own the strategy and evolution of Dropbox’s GTM technology ecosystem, ensuring systems, data architecture, automation, and workflows scale effectively with the business
Lead the adoption of AI across Revenue Operations and the broader GTM organization, identifying opportunities to fundamentally improve seller productivity, forecasting, customer targeting, planning, decision-making, and operational efficiency
Simplify legacy processes, systems, and organizational structures where needed, driving enterprise-wide transformation and change management across a complex stakeholder environment
Build, lead, and develop a world-class Revenue Operations organization, including strong leadership succession, organizational design, talent development, and clear accountability across teams
Represent Revenue Operations in executive-level and company-wide planning forums, serving as a trusted voice on GTM performance, risks, opportunities, and operating priorities
Requirements
15+ years of experience in Revenue Operations, Sales Operations, GTM Operations, Finance, or related functions within a scaled SaaS or product-led growth company
Proven experience leading revenue operations for multi-segment B2B organizations across SMB, Mid-Market, and Enterprise customer segments
Deep expertise in forecasting, revenue planning, territory design, quota management, sales productivity, incentive compensation, and pipeline management
Strong understanding of SaaS business models, revenue motions, and key operational drivers across acquisition, expansion, and retention
Demonstrated ability to identify operational challenges and implement scalable, data-driven solutions that improve business performance
Strong cross-functional leadership skills with the ability to influence senior stakeholders across Sales, Finance, Product, Marketing, and Customer organizations
Experience supporting organizations through significant growth and GTM evolution, including PLG, sales-assisted, and enterprise motions
Strong analytical and problem-solving capabilities, with extensive experience leveraging data to drive operational and business decisions
Track record of building, developing, and leading high-performing revenue operations teams in a fast-paced, evolving environment
Preferred Qualifications
Proven track record implementing AI-enabled revenue solutions that deliver measurable improvements in efficiency, productivity, and business outcomes
Demonstrated experience owning and scaling GTM technology ecosystems, with deep expertise in Salesforce and related revenue platforms
