About Akuna:
Akuna Capital  is an innovative  trading firm with a strong focus on collaboration,  cutting-edge  technology, data driven solutions , and automation. We specialize in providing liquidity as an options market maker – meaning we are committed to providing competitive quotes that we are willing to both buy and sell. To do this successfully ,  we design and implement our own low latency technologies, trading strategies ,  and mathematical models.
Our Founding Partners   first conceptualized Akuna in their hometown of Sydney. They opened the firm’s first office in 2011 in the heart of the derivatives industry and the options capital of the world – Chicago. Today, Akuna is proud to  operate  from  additional  offices in Sydney, Shanghai,  London,  and Singapore .
What you’ll do as a Platform Engineer at Akuna:
This is an exciting role where you will be given the opportunity to take ownership of projects and make them your own. At Akuna, we are constantly looking for ways to improve our efficiency as we continue to grow. As a Platform Engineer, you will work closely with our Trading, Development, Infrastructure, and Operations teams. You will design and implement cloud solutions, automate infrastructure as code, improve cloud governance and security, and monitor our hybrid-cloud environments. I n this role, you will:
Ensure cloud services are leveraged to their full potential (AWS, Azure, Google Cloud) and best practices are being followed
Help manage our public cloud growth and continuously improve Akuna’s hybrid-cloud operating model
Evaluate proposed solution architectures and provide recommendations
Provision, configure, upgrade, and monitor technologies such as Kubernetes (EKS and on-premise), Spark, Kafka, and other large-scale distributed databases
Automate infrastructure management and development pipelines with tools like Terraform, Ansible, Jenkins and GitLab
Explore new technologies in the cloud-native/container ecosystem
Work with Infrastructure and Development teams to monitor systems, clusters and cloud deployments to detect errors and monitor performance
Adhere to the engineering mindset of creating and maintaining performant and reliable production systems

Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field
3+ years of experience in Platform Engineering, Cloud Engineering, SRE, DevOps, or related infrastructure roles
This role is available hybrid from our Chicago office or fully remote.

Experience building and operating highly available, scalable, and secure cloud-native platforms
