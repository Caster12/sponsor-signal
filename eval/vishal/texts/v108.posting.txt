On a typical day, you might expect to lead complex technical projects, streamline interconnected business processes and technology environments, or use a variety of languages and platforms to architect and implement custom, scalable solutions for our clients. You will work alongside clients and interface with executive stakeholders to plan and execute successful engagements. Internally, you will collaborate with other Valent leaders in leading the operations of our business and contribute directly to the growth of Valent as a destination place to build one’s career.
