Quality Assurance Analyst
About AMEND:
AMEND is a management consulting firm based in Cincinnati, OH with areas of focus in operations, analytics, and technology.  We are focused on strengthening the people, processes, and systems in organizations to generate a holistic transformation. Our three-tiered approach provides a distinct competitive edge and allows us to build strong relationships and create customized solutions for every client.  This is an incredible time to step into a growing team where everyone is aligned to a common goal to change lives, transform businesses, and make a positive impact on anything we touch.
Overview:
Join AMEND as a Quality Assurance Analyst to gain hands-on experience supporting our Delivery team.  Our Quality Assurance Analyst will support delivery operations through data governance, project auditing, and operational oversight. This role involves auditing delivery metrics, organizing operational documentation, cleaning project data, and assisting with client revenue analysis.
This is a co-op/internship experience that includes the opportunity to participate in AMEND’s Analyst Program, a semester-long development program with curriculum to build a wholistic understanding of business. This is an incredible opportunity to be exposed to the world-class talent of AMEND and experience a transformational rotation with us!
This is an internal role and reports to our Project Controls Manager.
Key Responsibilities:
Delivery Metrics & Data Governance
Audit and ensure adherence to delivery metrics and data standards (e.g., Project Health Pulses and AMEND Quality System preparation).
Collect and verify baseline data related to project scope changes (Extension vs Expansion).
Support creation of Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) for delivery metric capture.
Manage and oversee the Operational SharePoint site: gather, organize, and track document artifacts with revision histories.
Data Cleanup and Analysis
Perform data cleanup in project management systems (e.g., Kantata), focusing on agreed definitions.
Audit Kantata projects to verify billing milestones, resourcing, and status accuracy.
Collect, analyze and clean historical data to support BI reporting comparisons.
Qualifications:
Pursuit of a degree in Business or a related field
Ability to build relationships and collaborate with AMEND team members
Comfort in using Excel and willingness to learn business intelligence tools (Power BI)
Basic familiarity or willingness to learn key operational business systems such as Kantata and Sharepoint
Good communication and ability to work independently with guidance
Strong organizational skills with excellent attention to detail
Preferred Attributes:
Proactive mindset, with the ability to take initiative and prioritize in a fast-paced, dynamic environment
Comfort in ambiguity and adaptability as tasks and direction continually evolve
Detail-focused and able to maintain data accuracy
Self-motivated and eager to learn new processes and tools, and to seek constant feedback
Motivation and excitement to drive your continued development.
