About The Role

We’re looking for a highly motivated Data Scientist with strong SQL expertise to support analytics, measurement, and operational insights across our human data training workflows. You’ll work with messy, fast-moving data related to expert performance, task quality, throughput, and overall operational efficiency.

This is a hands-on role with real ownership, close partnership with product and operations, and a steep growth curve for someone who executes well.

What You’ll Do

* Write advanced SQL to analyze large, complex datasets related to expert performance, task quality, and operational workflows.
* Define, build, and maintain core metrics that measure data quality, throughput, productivity, and operational efficiency.
* Partner with Product, Operations, and cross-functional stakeholders to answer high-impact business and product questions.
* Conduct analyses that inform workflow design, task improvements, and operational decision-making.
* Build dashboards, reporting, and self-serve analytics that enable fast, informed decisions.
* Identify data gaps, inconsistencies, and quality issues, then partner with relevant teams to resolve them.
* Translate ambiguous business questions into clear analyses and actionable recommendations.
* Communicate findings and recommendations clearly to both technical and non-technical stakeholders.

Desired Capabilities

* 3–5 years of experience in Data Science, Product Analytics, Business Intelligence, or a related analytical role.
* Expert-level SQL skills and experience working with large, complex datasets.
* Strong analytical judgment and comfort working with imperfect, real-world data.
* Experience defining metrics and using data to influence product or business decisions.
* High ownership mindset and ability to independently drive complex work forward.
* Strong communication skills and comfort partnering cross-functionally in a fast-paced environment.

Extra Credit

* Python experience for data analysis, such as pandas and notebooks.
* Experience supporting operational, marketplace, quality, or workforce-related analytics.
* Experience with experimentation, A/B testing, or metrics-driven product development.
* Exposure to AI data workflows, data labeling, or evaluation processes.
* Experience with analytics engineering, including building and maintaining DBT models.
