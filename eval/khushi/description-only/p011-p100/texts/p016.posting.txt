About Figure

Figure Technology Solutions, Inc. (Nasdaq: FIGR; OPEN: FGRS) is the leading blockchain-native capital marketplace for the origination, funding, sale and trading of tokenized assets. More than 480 partners use its loan origination system and capital marketplace. Collectively, Figure and its partners have originated over $30 billion of loans to date, among other products. The fastest growing components are Figure Connect, its credit marketplace, and Democratized Prime, Figure’s on-chain lend-borrow marketplace. Figure’s ecosystem also includes DART (Digital Asset Registry Technology) for asset custody and lien perfection, and $YLDS, a yield-bearing stablecoin registered with the SEC as a security under the Securities Act of 1933.

Figure is the market leader in real-world asset (RWA) tokenization. The company has received triple-A ratings from S&P and Moody’s on multiple loan securitizations, the first of its kind for blockchain finance.

We’re proud to be recognized as one of Forbes’ Top 50 Fintech Companies and CNBC’s World’s Top Fintech Companies.

About the Role

As a Senior Pricing Analyst, you'll support the operational and analytical backbone of Figure's pricing function, encompassing consumer loan pricing and commercial pricing models. Working closely with the Pricing & Revenue Strategy Lead, you'll execute day-to-day pricing operations and assist in customizing pricing strategies across Figure's partner network. This is a hands-on role suited for someone who wants to build deep fluency in loan pricing mechanics while contributing to a growing strategic function.

What You'll Do

Own execution of loan pricing changes and updates across channels and partners, working across pricing grids, systems, and internal tools

Monitor pricing accuracy, develop controls, and flag discrepancies or errors across pricing grids and automated workflows

Analyze product performance, funnel conversion, and competitor pricing to support pricing optimization efforts

Build and maintain dashboards and reporting to track key pricing and revenue metrics, including competitor pricing reporting

Support ad hoc financial and market analysis requests from Credit, Product, Marketing, and Go-to-Market teams

Document pricing processes and help maintain clear, accurate records of pricing decisions and their rationale

What We Look For

2-4 years of experience in data analytics, capital markets, loan pricing, credit risk, FP&A, or a related analytical role, ideally within fintech or financial services

Advanced analytical skills with proficiency in Excel, SQL, and Python; experience with dashboarding tools such as Tableau

Experience with D2C pricing; AB testing

Proven ability to translate complex data into actionable strategy and effectively communicate insights to diverse audiences

Experience collaborating with cross-functional teams 

Strong organizational and communication skills

Self-starter comfortable with ambiguity and capable of independently driving initiatives

Bachelor's degree in a quantitative field (e.g., Economics, Finance, Math, Engineering); advanced degree a plus

Adhere to all company security policies and data handling procedures.

Complete mandatory security awareness training within required timeframes.

Promptly report any suspected security incidents or suspicious activity to the Security team.
