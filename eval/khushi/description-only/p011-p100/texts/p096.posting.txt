Job Description
PA's Growth Strategy team helps organizations identify opportunities for sustainable growth through customer insight, market analysis, and strategic thinking. As a Consultant Analyst you will work alongside experienced practitioners to solve complex business challenges and help clients make confident strategic decisions.  

As a Growth Strategy Consultant Analyst at PA, you will: 

- Conduct quantitative and qualitative research to support client recommendations.  

- Analyze customer, market, financial, and business data to uncover growth opportunities.  

- Support the development of strategic recommendations and business cases through evidence-based hypotheses.  

- Participate in interviews, workshops, and collaborative problem-solving sessions to create clear, compelling presentations and deliverables for internal and client audiences.  

- Contribute curiosity, creativity, and analytical rigor to project teams. 

- Be welcomed to build office and company culture through the involvement in employee resource groups and firm initiatives. 

Qualifications
We're looking for individuals who are curious, collaborative, and motivated to solve complex challenges.

Successful candidates will typically demonstrate:  

Strong problem-solving and analytical skills. You’re able to simplify complex problems and ideas through structured analysis and clear thinking. 

Comfort with conducting quantitative and qualitative research.  

Excellent written and verbal communication abilities with the ability to collaborate effectively across teams, disciplines, and seniority-levels.  

Initiative, adaptability, and comfort working in dynamic environments.  

Commitment to inclusive teamwork and respect for diverse perspectives.  

Your background includes:  

Bachelor's or Master's degree  

1–4 years of relevant professional experience

Experience with Excel, PowerPoint, and Word required.   

Experience with research, analytics, AI tools, coding, leadership activities, apprenticeships, work experience, or community involvement is a plus.
