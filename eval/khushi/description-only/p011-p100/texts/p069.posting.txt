About the Role

The core of Mixpanel’s value to customers has always been great data. We’re in an exciting moment for the way our users are thinking about and working with product data, and we need help figuring out how our position in the data infrastructure stack evolves. That’s why we’re hiring a fantastic Product Manager for Mixpanel Data.

In this role, you'll own Mixpanel's strategy and roadmap for where we sit in the data landscape - how our data architecture evolves, where we build versus integrate versus get out of the way, and what it means for Mixpanel to work with data that increasingly lives outside our walls as well as inside them. This is a pathfinding role: a large part of the job is turning ambiguous technical and business tradeoffs into a clear point of view, a roadmap, and decisions the engineering and leadership team can act on.

What You'll Do

Define and drive Mixpanel's strategy and roadmap for how the product evolves alongside customers' data warehouses, lakes, and the broader modern data stack

Turn complex, ambiguous architecture and business tradeoffs into a clear recommendation, grounded in customer evidence, competitive context, and business impact

Partner closely with engineering leadership on build-vs-integrate-vs-partner decisions that shape Mixpanel's data architecture for years to come

Talk directly to customers and prospects to understand how their data infrastructure and workflows are changing, and translate that into product bets

Build the business case for infrastructure-level investments, making the ROI of foundational, long-horizon work legible to leadership

Define how Mixpanel's data model, governance, and metadata need to evolve to support deeper analysis and a growing product portfolio

Align engineering, design, and go-to-market teams around a shared strategic point of view, with clear, written communication

Define and track the metrics that tell you whether the strategy is working, and course-correct as new evidence comes in

What We're Looking For

5+ years of hands-on product management experience, including ownership of technically complex, infrastructure- or platform-level problems

Direct experience with the modern data stack — data warehouses, data lakes, ETL/ELT, or data platform tooling — as a builder, user, or product owner

Comfort operating in ambiguity, with a track record of synthesizing competing technical and business inputs into a clear strategic point of view

Experience partnering closely with engineering on architecture-level tradeoffs, not just feature scoping

Strong written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to bring technical and non-technical audiences along on a strategic narrative

Experience using AI tools in product management workflows (e.g., competitive research, prototyping)

Nice to Have

Experience at a data infrastructure, data platform, or analytics company

Hands-on experience with data warehouses/lakehouses (e.g., Snowflake, BigQuery, Databricks) or open data formats

Experience writing production-grade software

Experience designing products using tools like Figma, Sketch, or Abstract
