About the role 

We’re seeking a senior lifecycle and CRM leader to build a coordinated customer-growth capability for a digital-presence services business serving entrepreneurs, small businesses, freelancers, and customers building and managing an online presence. The portfolio spans domain registration, website creation, web hosting, professional email, security, and digital marketing solutions. 

The business is evolving from product- and campaign-led communications toward a more integrated lifecycle model. You will assess the current state, define the highest-value customer journeys, and establish a practical roadmap connecting customer and account data, setup and service signals, marketing technology, content, measurement, and cross-functional execution. 

The work should help customers move from purchasing a domain or digital service to successfully establishing, activating, managing, protecting, and growing their online presence. It should strengthen service activation, time to value, complementary-service adoption, renewal readiness, retention, expansion, and win-back. You will serve as a senior advisor while remaining hands-on in shaping the first wave of journeys, tests, and operating practices. 

What You’ll Do 

• Assess the end-to-end digital-presence lifecycle. Map the experience from domain search and purchase through account setup, website creation and launch, hosting and professional email activation, security adoption, ongoing management, renewal, cancellation or lapse, win-back, and expansion into relevant adjacent services. 

• Build the lifecycle strategy and roadmap. Define lifecycle stages, customer and business outcomes, journey priorities, and a sequenced plan balancing immediate improvements with longer-term data, technology, content, and operating-model needs. 

• Design high-value customer journeys. Develop coordinated programs for onboarding, service activation, website launch, customer education, renewal preparation, lapse prevention, retention, service recovery, win-back, and expansion into complementary digital-presence services. 

• Advance segmentation and trigger strategy. Define actionable audiences and triggers using customer profile, domain and service purchases, setup milestones, website launch status, account activity, billing and renewal status, support interactions, engagement, and service-portfolio signals. 

• Connect CRM, service, and channel data. Determine the data, identity, consent, automation, and integration capabilities required to coordinate experiences across email, web, account experiences, service interactions, and other available channels. 

• Improve personalization and next-best-action decisions. Deliver relevant setup guidance, website-launch support, education, security and renewal reminders, complementary-service recommendations, and re-engagement based on where customers are in building and managing their online presence. 

• Establish experimentation and measurement. Define baselines, test plans, controls, and reporting for setup completion, service activation, website launch, time to value, multi-service relationships, engagement, renewal, lapse prevention, retention, win-back, customer value, and incremental commercial impact. 

• Create the lifecycle operating model. Clarify decision rights, ownership, intake, prioritization, approvals, production, reporting, and optimization across marketing, product, ecommerce, customer care, data, analytics, creative, and technology. 

• Move the first wave into delivery. Translate recommendations into journey maps, briefs, audience and trigger definitions, content requirements, test designs, measurement plans, and a prioritized delivery backlog. 

• Build sustainable internal capability. Create reusable frameworks, governance, documentation, and working rhythms that enable internal teams to continuously improve lifecycle programs. 

What Success Looks Like

 

• A clear lifecycle and CRM maturity assessment covering journeys, data, segmentation, triggers, channels, technology, content, measurement, and team capability. 

• A prioritized roadmap tied to customers establishing, activating, managing, protecting, and growing their online presence, with measurable outcomes across time to value, renewal, retention, expansion, and win-back. 

• A defined first wave of journeys with owners, requirements, dependencies, tests, and measurement. 

• A more connected experience for customers holding one or multiple services across domains, websites, hosting, professional email, security, digital marketing, billing, renewals, and support. 

• Clear governance and operating rhythms across the functions supporting the customer experience. 

• Internal teams equipped to sustain and improve the lifecycle capability.
