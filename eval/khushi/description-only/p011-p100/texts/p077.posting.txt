Show Us Your Influence

We want to see how your enthusiasm for AI translates into discoveries other people use. If your résumé references that you are leading or contributing to AI product communities on Discord, Reddit, YouTube, TikTok, X, or other platforms, please include your handles, profile or community links, and specific examples of your contributions. If you are referencing demos, tutorials, conversations, or workflows people follow you for, please include those links or evidence that others engage with, adopt, or build on your discoveries. For private communities, screenshots with other members’ identifying information removed are welcome. Existing work only; no new projects are required.

A large following isn’t essential. Demonstrated product expertise, trusted participation, and meaningful influence are.

About the Role 

You live and breathe frontier AI. You’re testing new releases, pushing agents beyond their obvious uses, and discovering possibilities you can’t wait to show other people. This is how you spend your time because you love it. You’ve already brought others along through a community, channel, blog, or the conversations where people turn to you for ideas and advice. 

Sia Experience’s Marketing Advisory is providing consulting to one of the world’s leading technology companies on go-to-market strategy to grow adoption of its next-generation consumer AI agent, and we’re looking for an AI Product Evangelist to help drive that growth. In this role, you’ll immerse yourself in the product, uncover unexpected applications, and show people how to get more from its capabilities. You’ll connect with other enthusiastic users, build on their discoveries, and turn individual experiments into ideas and resources that inspire broader adoption. 

You’ll bring firsthand knowledge of what users are building, what they’re discussing, and where the product could go next. Your insights should introduce the team to possibilities they haven’t explored and help translate emerging user behavior into meaningful product opportunities. 

Your background might be in product, engineering, creative technology, education, content, community, or another discipline. What matters most is your demonstrated ability to explore AI deeply, share what you discover, and get others involved. 

What You’ll Do 

Bring the exploration, discoveries, and connections you’re already making into a direct partnership with the team building the product. You may already be a power user of this product, or bring deep experience with another frontier AI agent and the curiosity to quickly become one. This role gives you the opportunity to expand that work, involve the internal team, and help more people discover what’s possible. 

Discover and Demonstrate What’s Possible 

• Build on your existing product knowledge and experiments, exploring new features, integrations, and agent capabilities as they emerge. 

• Expand the practical, creative, and unexpected use cases you’re already discovering, bringing the team into what you’re testing and learning. 

• Push beyond established examples to uncover new workflows and applications, collaborating with Product and Engineering to explore their potential. 

• Turn the discoveries you already share into compelling demos, tutorials, posts, and reusable resources that reach more people. 

• Bring your ongoing immersion in frontier AI into the team’s thinking, sharing emerging capabilities, relevant developments, and informed user perspectives. 

Inspire and Connect Users 

• Deepen the conversations and relationships you already have in Reddit threads, Discord channels, and other spaces where AI enthusiasts exchange ideas and build together. 

• Extend the trust you’ve earned through useful contributions, firsthand product knowledge, and helping others experiment. 

• Bring inventive users and compelling projects to the team’s attention, connecting people who could learn from or collaborate with one another. 

• Build on the ways you already get others involved through shared experiments, demonstrations, challenges, and collaborative activities. 

Qualifications
• Several years of being the product geek who tests every release, finds the unexpected use case, and gets everyone else saying, “Wait, how did you do that?” Your experience with frontier AI may be more recent, but your instinct to explore runs deep. 

• Demonstrated hands-on experience with AI agents or advanced AI platforms, including building tools, executing complex workflows, or automating tasks beyond conversational prompting. 

• A portfolio of projects, demos, tutorials, workflows, or public contributions that demonstrates product fluency and inventive applications. 

• Experience building or actively contributing to an AI or technology community, with evidence that others engage with, learn from, or build on your contributions. 

• Ability to create clear, compelling content that helps users understand capabilities, reproduce use cases, and get more value from a product. 

• Experience identifying user needs, product limitations, and emerging behaviors, and translating those observations into actionable recommendations. 

• Practical knowledge of using agents, bots, integrations, or automation to support community participation and reduce repetitive work. 

• Strong written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to engage enthusiast communities authentically and collaborate effectively with Product, Engineering, and Marketing. 

• Ability to independently prioritize work, test ideas, follow through on commitments, and adapt as product capabilities evolve. 

Direct experience as a power user of the product is strongly preferred. Deep experience with another frontier AI agent or platform is also relevant. A formal community management title is not required.
