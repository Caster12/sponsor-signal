Senior Staff Software Engineer, Developer Productivity
Asana
New York City

We are looking for a Senior Staff Software Engineer to play a key leadership role in our Developer Productivity group and drive developer velocity and fundamentally change the software development lifecycle with AI. The scope spans across the stack including tooling, frameworks, and infrastructure to provide full leverage in the developer experience for every software developer at Asana. Some of the teams’ current work include building out our internal agentic harness, setting up our online/offline developer evals, and increasing the effectiveness of our automated validation loop. The importance and scope of this work has grown significantly in the last year, and we need someone operating at the group level to help sequence and execute it.

This role can be based in our New York or San Francisco offices with an office-centric hybrid schedule. The standard in-office days are Monday, Tuesday, and Thursday. Most Asanas have the option to work from home on Wednesdays. Working from home on Fridays depends on the type of work you do and the teams with which you partner. If you're interviewing for this role, your recruiter will share more about the in-office requirements.

What you’ll achieve

Drive critical developer productivity and AI developer tooling projects that impact 500+ developers at Asana

Partner with the Developer Productivity Group Tech Lead to define technical vision and strategy for 6+ engineering teams, all focused towards improving velocity for Asana engineers

Build and align on the roadmap for Asana's development platform to drive faster development lifecycle feedback loops and uplevel how every Asana engineer ships

Own key outcomes like extending the internal tooling engineers use to delegate work to agents across harnesses

Mentor senior and staff-level engineers in Developer Productivity and across Foundations and the rest of the Engineering organization

Act as a resource for team-level tech leads on topics like longer-term technical planning, design & architecture, and prioritizing tech debt

About you

10+ years of experience working as a technical contributor in large-scale products

2+ years of experience at owning a build system, developer environment, CI or equivalent at an organization of 200+ engineers

Experience making and delegating technical decisions that have org-wide impact

Developed a fluency in current AI developer tooling and have strong enough opinions to collaborate with teams and set direction

Passionate about coaching and mentoring engineers of all experience levels

Deep care for collaboration and an ability to foster an inclusive culture where everyone can bring their full selves to work and thrive

Demonstrates curiosity about AI tools and emerging technologies, with a willingness to learn and leverage them to enhance productivity, collaboration, or decision-making

At Asana, we're committed to building teams that include a variety of backgrounds, perspectives, and skills, as this is critical to helping us achieve our mission. If you're interested in this role and don't meet every listed requirement, we still encourage you to apply.
