About Upstart

At Upstart, we’re united by a mission that matters: to radically reduce the cost and complexity of borrowing for all Americans. Every day, we bring creativity, experimentation, and advanced AI to reshape access to credit, helping millions move forward financially with clarity and confidence.

As the leading AI lending marketplace, we partner with banks and credit unions to expand access to affordable credit through technology that’s both radically intelligent and deeply human. Our platform runs over one million predictions per borrower using more than 3,000 signals, powering smarter, fairer decisions for millions of customers. But the numbers only hint at the impact. Every idea, every voice, and every contribution moves us closer to a world where credit never stands between people and their financial progress.

And whether you choose to work primarily from home or collaborate in-person from one of our offices in Columbus, Austin, the Bay Area, or New York City, you’ll have the support to work in the way that works best for you.

If you’re energized by tackling meaningful problems, excited to innovate with purpose, and motivated by work that truly matters, we’d love to hear from you.

The Team: 

Upstart’s Credit Analytics team is at the center of how we understand, forecast, and communicate credit performance across the company. The team builds the frameworks, infrastructure, and insights that connect our AI-driven underwriting, portfolio performance, and funding strategy, ensuring decisions are grounded in a rigorous and forward-looking view of risk and return.

As a Staff Credit Analyst, you will contribute to high-impact credit analytics supporting portfolio strategy, financial planning, and funding decisions. You will work closely with Machine Learning, Finance, Product, Engineering, and Capital teams to translate complex credit dynamics into actionable insights and scalable analytical solutions.

This role offers the opportunity to deepen expertise across credit forecasting and valuation, portfolio analytics, and risk capital analytics while helping improve the tools and processes used to monitor and manage credit performance.

How you’ll make an impact

Develop and maintain credit analytics used for portfolio monitoring, forecasting, valuation, and decision-making.

Support loss forecasting, scenario analysis, portfolio performance measurement, and related reporting.

Contribute to risk capital analytics for funding strategies, including securitizations, warehouse facilities, and forward-flow programs.

Analyze portfolio trends and translate findings into clear recommendations for product, credit policy, finance, and funding stakeholders.

Partner with cross-functional teams to align on definitions, metrics, analytical frameworks, and business requirements.

Build and improve data models, pipelines, dashboards, and automated reporting to enable reliable, self-serve insights.

Establish strong practices for analytical validation, monitoring, documentation, and governance.

Communicate complex analyses clearly to both technical and non-technical audiences.

Minimum Qualifications 

5+ years of experience in analytics, credit risk, quantitative finance, or a related field within fintech, financial services, consumer lending, or a similar industry.

Experience with credit risk, loss forecasting, portfolio analytics, valuation, or related financial-performance frameworks.

Working knowledge of risk capital or structured finance concepts, such as securitizations, warehouse facilities, forward-flow programs, or investor reporting.

Strong SQL skills and proficiency in at least one programming language, such as Python or R.

Experience working with large datasets and developing repeatable, well-documented analyses.

Ability to turn complex analytical work into clear, actionable business insights.

Strong written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to collaborate effectively across functions.

Preferred Qualifications

Experience partnering with Machine Learning or Data Science teams, particularly in underwriting or model-performance monitoring.

Familiarity with capital analytics, deal economics, and risk/return frameworks.

Experience building analytics infrastructure, dashboards, automated reporting, or GenAI-enabled analytical solutions.

Exposure to model governance, controls, audit, model validation, or valuation review processes.

Demonstrated ability to manage analytical projects, influence stakeholders, and drive alignment.
