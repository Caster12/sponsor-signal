# Provenance

All 90 labels in `labels/khushi.jsonl` were made and reviewed by Khushi from the saved employer descriptions. AI was used only to format the files (the worksheet, the JSONL and this folder's layout), not to decide labels.

`seen_model_output: false` means no extension prediction was consulted while labeling. These are one labeler's labels: they have not been double-labeled or adjudicated yet, so agreement (Cohen's kappa) cannot be reported for them.

Seven cases are flagged for adjudication (p046, p051, p052, p059, p060, p063, p065), including four conditional export-control notices where the codebook needs a decision. No positive sponsorship statements were identified in this batch.
